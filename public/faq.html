Frequently asked questions

What does ResuNest do?

ResuNest reads the selectable text in a resume PDF, identifies skills, and suggests career fields and roles based on skill overlap.

Do I need an account?

No. ResuNest is available without sign-up.

What resume files are supported?

Use a PDF with selectable text. Image-only or scanned PDFs may need OCR before they can be analyzed accurately.

How many analyses can I run?

Each visitor can run up to five PDF analyses in a three-hour window.

Are the results hiring advice?

No. Results are starting points for career exploration, not hiring decisions or guarantees.
