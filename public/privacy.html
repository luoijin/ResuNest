Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Resume analysis

Your selected PDF stays in your browser. ResuNest extracts its text in the browser and sends that text to the ResuNest API and Gemini to identify skills. The original PDF file is not stored by ResuNest.

Usage limits

To enforce the anonymous analysis limit, ResuNest stores a salted hash of a visitor's IP address with a usage counter and expiry time. It does not store the plain IP address for this purpose.

Local history

Recent analyses are saved in your browser storage. You can remove them in the app or by clearing browser site data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ResuNest team through an email address listed in the site footer.
