ResuNest documentation

How it works

Upload a resume PDF with selectable text. ResuNest extracts skills, compares them with its career catalog, and presents job matches and learning recommendations.

Using your results

Select a job match to see missing skills and learning resources. You can download the current analysis as a PDF or reopen a saved local analysis from Recent analyses.

Usage limit

Visitors can run up to five PDF analyses during each three-hour window.

Privacy

Your PDF remains in the browser. Its extracted text is sent for AI skill analysis. See the Privacy Policy for details.
